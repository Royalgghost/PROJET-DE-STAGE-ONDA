Deviation log — template adaptation

4. The source template’s “Liceria & Co.” labels and generic website text are removed because they do not belong to the ONDA project.
5. French project content is used to remain consistent with the internship report; inherited English headings are rewritten in place.
6. Metrics are kept concise and rounded only where the template’s inherited text boxes require compact presentation copy. The full methodology remains in the report.
7. No new master, layout, or unrelated slide family is introduced. The CyberRange is described as an isolated and authorized simulation environment.
